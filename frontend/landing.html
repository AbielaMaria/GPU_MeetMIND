Features

Built for the whole meeting lifecycle

From the first word spoken to the follow-up you send afterwards.

Real-time transcription

Stream your mic straight to the server and watch the transcript build as people talk — no upload, no waiting.

AI-generated summaries

One click turns a wall of transcript into a titled brief with objective, key points, and outcomes.

Speaker detection

Attribute lines to distinct voices so the record reads like a conversation, not a monologue.

Searchable history

Every meeting is saved and full-text searchable, so "what did we decide about pricing?" takes seconds.

Exportable notes

Send the summary and action items to Markdown, PDF, or your inbox without reformatting a thing.

Integrations

Push results into Slack, Notion, Jira, and calendar invites.

How it works

Three steps, start to insight

  1. 1

    Record

    Hit record when the meeting starts. MeetMind streams clean, mono audio to the server in real time.

  2. 2

    Transcribe

    When you stop, a Whisper-class model returns the full transcript in seconds, ready to read or edit.

  3. 3

    Get insights

    Click Create Summary and an LLM extracts the title, summary, decisions, tasks, and action items.
